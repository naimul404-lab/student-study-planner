# Accessibility and inclusive design

This project aims to make planning usable for people with different needs and study styles.

## Design goals

- Use plain language and avoid requiring users to understand productivity jargon.
- Keep commands and screens short, predictable, and easy to navigate with a keyboard.
- Do not use color as the only way to communicate status; include words such as `Due soon` or `Complete`.
- Let users choose priorities and study durations that fit their circumstances.
- Treat suggested tasks as optional guidance, not a judgment or a rigid schedule.
- Encourage breaks and realistic plans.

## Feedback

If something makes the project difficult to use, please open an issue describing the problem and your preferred way to interact with it. Avoid sharing private health or personal information in public issues.
