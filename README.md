# Student Study Planner

A free, beginner-friendly Python command-line planner to help students organize assignments, exams, and study sessions without needing an account or internet connection.

> **Plan your next study session, not your whole life.** Add a task, choose a due date, and take one manageable step at a time.

## Why this project?

Deadlines and study tasks can feel overwhelming when they are scattered across notes and messages. This project aims to give students one simple place to record what needs doing and decide what to focus on next.

It is designed to be:

- **Free and offline** — runs locally on your computer.
- **Private by default** — your study data stays in a local file.
- **Simple to use** — a clear command-line interface, no account needed.
- **Beginner-friendly** — small, readable Python code that learners can explore and improve.

## Planned features

- Add assignments, exams, and study tasks.
- Record a subject, due date, and estimated study time.
- View upcoming and overdue tasks.
- Mark completed tasks and remove items.
- Suggest a manageable next task based on deadline and priority.
- Save tasks locally so they remain available after restarting.
- Include optional sample tasks so new users can try it quickly.

> Features are planned; the working implementation and setup instructions will be added as development progresses.

## Who can benefit?

- Students who want a simple way to track school or university work.
- Learners who prefer an offline tool without accounts or subscriptions.
- New Python programmers who want to study a practical project.
- Contributors looking for small, clearly scoped improvements.

## Privacy and wellbeing

This planner is a basic organizational aid, not a medical, counseling, or academic-advising service. It should help break work into manageable tasks—not encourage all-nighters or unrealistic schedules. Keep breaks and personal needs in your plan. Data is intended to be stored locally; do not put sensitive personal information in task notes.
